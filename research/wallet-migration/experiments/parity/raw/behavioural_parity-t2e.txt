## Finding: melt-quote parity is NOT available through WalletPort
gonuts' adapter returns: "RequestMeltQuote: not yet wired; TollWallet uses
MeltToLightning at a higher level". cdkinterop exposes no melt subcommand
(the CDK daemon does). So port-level melt parity is N/A; any melt comparison
must go through the higher-level MeltToLightning (LNURL), which needs real
Lightning. This is recorded, not asserted.

## Note
Both implementations emit V4 (`cashuB`) tokens on send; tokens are
interchanged successfully in both directions.
