## Note on the alternative (same-seed NUT-09 restore)
Not exercised: gonuts stores its BIP-39 mnemonic in its bbolt `wallet.db`
(`wallet.Mnemonic()`), but a separate export is needed (the store is locked
while the service runs, and LoadWallet blocks on mint registration), and
CDK/gonuts NUT-13 derivation-path compatibility is unverified. The token
transfer above is implementation-agnostic and proven (T2e).
