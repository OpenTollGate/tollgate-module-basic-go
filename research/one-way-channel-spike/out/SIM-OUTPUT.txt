T4  Customer's maximum value at risk per transaction (sats)
-----------------------------------------------------------
        purchase |          cashu |        channel |     chan/cashu
  ---------------+----------------+----------------+---------------
               1 |              1 |            717 |           717x
              64 |             64 |            780 |            12x
           1,000 |          1,000 |          1,716 |             2x
          10,000 |         10,000 |         10,716 |             1x
         100,000 |        100,000 |        100,716 |             1x
  Cashu exposes the customer to EXACTLY the amount being bought -- a bearer
  token is handed over like cash, so there is nothing else to lose. A
  one-way channel locks the entire prefund BEFORE any service is
  rendered. The channel INCREASES customer exposure by the whole
  settlement cost, and on top of that the escape hatch is only usable by
  a payer who can observe height and publish in time (demo_channel.py T5).

T5  What the channel replaces, and its own new costs
----------------------------------------------------
       mechanism |           unit |           cost |       who pays
  ---------------+----------------+----------------+---------------
  mint NUT-07 checkstate |         ~1 RTT |         ~0 sat | router (not the customer)
  Cashu swap NUT-03 |          0 bps |          0 sat | router, at all 5 configured mints
  Lightning melt payout |           0.1% |   10 / 10k sat | router; already exists today
  CHANNEL funding tx |         155 vB |        310 sat | NEW -- customer must bear
  CHANNEL claim tx |         203 vB |        406 sat | NEW -- once per channel
  The thing being removed costs ~0 sat and one round-trip. The thing
  replacing it costs hundreds of sats. This is a strictly worse trade
  at the product's own unit size.

A4 DOMINANCE VERDICT (computed, not asserted)
==============================================================================
  At a 1,000-sat purchase (the largest plausible single TollGate sale):
    Cashu cost           : 1.00 sats
    Channel cost         : 746 sats (71.6% overhead)
    To reach the 0.1% a mint/LN fee costs, a channel must be worth
    716,000 sats = 499.9 years of internet.

  VERDICT: Cashu DOMINATES the one-way channel for every purchase size
  this product sells. Cashu is a bearer instrument, needs no chain, lets
  the payer hand value over while fully offline, is free per purchase, and
  caps the CUSTOMER's exposure at the amount being bought. The channel adds
  on-chain cost, capital lockup, timelock and fee-timeliness risk, and a
  larger customer exposure -- and buys back only the removal of a mint
  round-trip that the customer was never performing anyway.

LIMITS OF THIS SIMULATOR (mandatory)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  IT CANNOT SUPPORT, and no claim is made about:
   - whether any construction is consensus-valid (-> demo_channel.py)
   - whether a construction is non-custodial (-> demo_channel.py T3/T5)
   - exploitability, breach races, reorg behaviour (-> not tested at all:
     regtest does not reorg on demand here, so A3's reorg risk is
     UNQUANTIFIED and argued from construction, not measured)
   - real market fee rates, mint fee schedules, or BTC price
   - real user behaviour (abandon rate, session length are guesses)
  IT IS a deterministic arithmetic model over SOURCED module constants
  and explicitly-listed ASSUMED parameters. Change the assumptions in
  ASSUMPTIONS and the tables move; the conclusion's DIRECTION does not,
  because the sign is set by the ratio in T1/T2 (three to four orders of
  magnitude), not by any single parameter.
