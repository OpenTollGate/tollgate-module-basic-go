============================================================================
granularity.py -- break-even for an on-chain channel at TollGate prices
============================================================================
  live config      : metric=bytes, step_size=22020096 B (21 MiB = 21 MiB exactly)
  price            : 1 sat per step
  => 1 satoshi buys: 21 MiB of data

T0  ERRATUM -- the two readings of step_size=22020096
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  as MILLISECONDS  : 22020096 ms = 6.117 h  (what the original sim assumed: '1 sat = 6.12 hours')
  as BYTES (LIVE)  : 22020096 B = 21 MiB  (what the shipped config means)
  the value 22020096 = 21 * 2**20 exactly, which is a clean
  MiB quantity -- evidence the bytes reading is the intended default.

T1  A realistic purchase, in sats (the denominator for amortisation)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
      data bought |      = sats | (= fiat @ $100k/BTC)
  1 month light (50 GB)  |       2,271 | ($     2.27)
  1 month heavy (300 GB) |      13,624 | ($    13.62)
  1 year heavy (3.6 TB)  |     163,487 | ($   163.49)
  1 year very heavy (12 TB) |     544,957 | ($   544.96)

T2  Channel overhead = 716 sat / purchase (ratio is metric-independent)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
        purchase |      = data |         overhead
          100 sat |    2.1 GiB |       716.00%
        1,000 sat |   20.5 GiB |        71.60%
        5,000 sat |  102.5 GiB |        14.32%
       10,000 sat |  205.1 GiB |         7.16%
       50,000 sat |   1.00 TiB |         1.43%
       71,600 sat |   1.43 TiB |         1.00%
      716,000 sat |  14.34 TiB |         0.10%

T3  Break-even expressed as TIME, at a given usage rate
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  (price is per BYTE, so 'time' depends on how hard you use the link)

     usage rate |  sats/month |  months to 5% | months to 1% | to 0.1%
  light  50 GB/mo  |       2,271 |          6.31 |        31.53 | 315.33
  heavy 300 GB/mo  |      13,624 |          1.05 |         5.26 |  52.55
  very heavy 1 TB/mo |      45,413 |          0.32 |         1.58 |  15.77

T4  Cashu vs channel, at the CORRECTED units
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Cashu cost per purchase = 0.1% (melt). Channel = 716 sat fixed.

     purchase |  cashu 0.1% | channel 716 |  channel / cashu
        1,000 sat |         1.0 |         716 |         716.0x
       10,000 sat |        10.0 |         716 |          71.6x
       71,600 sat |        71.6 |         716 |          10.0x
      716,000 sat |       716.0 |         716 |           1.0x

  NOTE: at the corrected (bytes) units the channel is 1-3 orders of
  magnitude worse, NOT the 3-4 orders the ms-metric sim reported. The
  cost argument is therefore WEAKER than first published. The CUSTODY
  argument (V0/V2/V3 always carry a custodian) is unaffected at any price.
