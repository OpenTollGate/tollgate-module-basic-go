## Why failure (1) matters
1-leaf and 2-leaf trees agreed with Core. Only the 3-leaf tree was wrong.
A flat, balanced 3-leaf root computes br(br(hA,hB),br(hC,hC)) by promoting the
odd leaf. Core's descriptor was `tr(K,{pk(A),{pk(B),pk(C)}})`, which is a NESTED
tree: br(hA, br(hB,hC)). Different shape -> different root -> different key.
The API could not express the shape, which was the real defect: `MerkleRoot(flat
list)` silently assumed balance.

## Failure (2) was a test bug, recorded rather than quietly fixed
`5120 || 32 bytes` IS a valid tapscript program, so `IsTaproot` was RIGHT to
accept it and the assertion was wrong. The assertion was corrected to test a
genuinely invalid shape (a 33-byte v1 program) instead of weakening the code.
