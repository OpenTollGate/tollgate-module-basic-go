+- Fixed the `wallet.backend` wiring gap: the merchant service now routes every wallet construction (full service and all three degraded-mode paths) through the `tollwallet.OpenWallet` selection seam, and config gains a `wallet` section (`backend`: `default` in-process or `cdk` sidecar; `socket`, default `/var/run/cdk-walletd.sock`). Legacy configs without the section keep the in-process wallet unchanged, `cdk` with a dead socket degrades wallet-less (fail closed) instead of silently constructing a second wallet implementation, and a typo'd backend is refused rather than treated as the default ([#1200](https://github.com/OpenTollGate/tollgate-module-basic-go/pull/1200))
