- **Operator-issued custom-unit ecash.** A gateway can now price and accept ecash in a unit its own mint issues (`mb`, `min`, or another known unit) instead of assuming sats: `tollwallet.Token` exposes `Unit()`, `MintConfig` carries a `unit`, and pricing refuses a token whose unit does not match the mint's configured price unit rather than silently valuing 64 mb as 64 sats. Adds `wallet issue <amount> <unit>` on the CLI and the daemon socket (`Merchant.IssueOnRequest`) for the operator side of the flow, and opens the CDK in-process wallet per mint unit. The gonuts path is unchanged for sat-denominated mints ([#1040](https://github.com/OpenTollGate/tollgate-module-basic-go/pull/1040))
