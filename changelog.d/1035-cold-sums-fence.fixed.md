- **Cold-build go.sum gaps are fenced.** A missing transitive in `go.sum` only surfaces when the module graph resolves without a warm build cache — which is how a broken `main` passed every lane while cold builders failed (#1036). The pre-commit hook now runs `go mod tidy -diff` per affected module (seconds, refuses incomplete or drifted sums), and `release-check` gains a cold-sums leg: the full battery against scratch module+build caches.
