- **Advertise the canonical Cashu price unit.** A `price_unit` of `sats` in `config.json` was published verbatim in the kind:10021 `price_per_step` tag, and a consumer comparing it against the `sat` inside the customer's token rejected valid e-cash — the captive portal reported the rejection as a mint keyset rotation, so the customer was told to fetch a fresh token that failed the same way. The advertisement now always carries the protocol's spelling, and the `price_unit` migration covers every non-canonical spelling rather than one literal ([#1128](https://github.com/OpenTollGate/tollgate-module-basic-go/pull/1128))
