- **Per-mint payment method for custom-unit mints.** Custom-unit issuance derived the mint's payment method from the *unit* and fell back to a hardcoded `"paypal"` — a bench-harness stub promoted to a shipped default. Against an operator mint whose processor registers any other name (Pecan registers `branch`) every issuance quote addressed a method the mint does not have, so issuance could never succeed; and because it was a per-process environment variable, one router could not front a counter-settled mint and a bolt11 mint at the same time. The method is now declared per mint as `accepted_mints[].payment_method`, an unconfigured custom-unit mint is refused with an actionable error instead of sent to a guess, and a sat mint is unaffected (empty means the unit's default).
