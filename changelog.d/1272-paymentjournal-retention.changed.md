- **`paymentjournal` can reclaim finished records, so its 1024-record cap bounds volume instead of lifetime.** `Prepare` refused once the store held `maxRecords` operations, counting every record whatever its state, with no prune or delete path anywhere — so any caller journalling one record per unit of business stopped being able to record anything after 1024 units, permanently and without an adversary. New `Store.Prune(Retention, now)` reclaims only records in a state that can never advance again (`Paid`, `Completed`, `Failed`); in-flight records are never touched regardless of age, because those states are what reconciliation and duplicate-suppression read. The zero `Retention` prunes nothing, so existing callers are unaffected until they opt in, and `KeepSettled: true` keeps settled records for callers whose only local proof a payment happened is that record. `Capacity()` and `OldestInFlight()` let a caller tell a journal full of finished work from one wedged by operations that never resolved.
